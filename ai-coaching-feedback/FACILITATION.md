# Facilitation Guide · Feedback, Ready

The full runbook lives in [`facilitator/notes.json`](facilitator/notes.json)
on the ATD framework (Say / Do / Ask / Debrief / Transition per section,
plus prep checklists, contingencies, tough questions, and templates).
Regenerate the facilitator edition after any change:

```bash
python3 tools/build-facilitator.py
```

**Pre-work:** bring one upcoming conversation you're not fully looking
forward to, held privately. **The norm:** roles, never names; nothing typed
leaves the learner's screen.

## Run of show, two paths

| Slide | Full (40 min) | Core (30 min) |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome / QR | 2 | 1 |
| Objectives | 1 | 1 |
| Agenda | 1 | skip |
| 01 Prep, not outsource | 8 | 6 |
| Manifesto | 1 | skip |
| 02 The feedback draft (lab) | 8 | 6 |
| 03 Signals, not surveillance | 8 | 6 |
| Recap quiz | 3 | 3 |
| My 1:1 Prep Card (capstone) | 5 | 5 |
| Glossary | 1 | skip |
| Closing / commitment | 2 | 2 |
| **Total** | **40** | **30** |

**Core-path rules:** both trainers, the lab, the recap, and the capstone
stay. Pair activities become solo passes; debriefs shrink to one voice.

**Safety rails:** roles never names, enforced warmly and immediately;
anything serious routes privately to HR or the EAP. The surveillance
boundary is answered with the two-question test, never with tool-shaming.

## Validation model

In the moment: trainer scores and the graded lab. End of session: the
4-question recap (L2), fist-to-five (L1), and the prep card with a spoken
role-and-day commitment. After: the 7-day pulse on prepped conversations
actually held (L3).
