Feedback, Ready: the cheat sheet

The line

  • Prep: rehearsal, structure, questions, pattern-spotting. AI is great at all of it.
  • Outsource: unread drafts sent, lines whispered mid-conversation. Never.
  • The test: did the AI leave you MORE present in the room, or less?

The data rules (no exceptions)

  • De-identify first: roles and situations, never names, before anything touches a tool.
  • Approved VU tools only for anything internal.
  • Red light: private information about people never goes into unapproved tools.
  • Behavior, not character: describe what happened, never who someone is.

The feedback prep prompt (steal this)

"Here are my rough observations about a situation with a team member (role only, de-identified). Structure them as Situation, Behavior, Impact. Flag anything that reads as a judgment of character rather than a description of behavior, and list the questions I should ask before assuming my read is right."

Then the rewrite pass: verify every example, rewrite in your voice, cut anything you wouldn't say to their face.

SBI in one breath

  1. Situation: when and where.
  2. Behavior: what you observed, camera-true.
  3. Impact: what it caused.

The tell: "two handoffs went out without the checklist" is discussable; "you're careless" is only defensible.

The rehearsal move

  1. De-identify the situation.
  2. "Play the other person; push back hard on me."
  3. Run it twice before it counts once.
  4. Close the laptop; have the real one human.

Signals: the boundary test

  • Q1: aggregate (or your own notes), or one person who hasn't asked?
  • Q2: would you tell the team you do this?
  • Survey themes into discussion questions: yes. Hunting who wrote what: never.
  • Your own 1:1 notes, de-identified, mined monthly: the best cheap habit here.

The numbers

  • Managers: about 50% of work AI-assisted; ICs about 34% (SHRM).
  • Coaching platforms (Culture Amp AI Coach, Lattice, 15Five) now embed AI in the flow of people work.
  • The differentiator is not access; it's the prep-not-outsource discipline.

My prep card, fill in the blanks

Conversation: [role + topic, no names]. Prep move: [rehearsal / SBI draft / notes mine / themes move]. I will NOT: [names in unapproved tools / unverified drafts / managing from a screen]. Prep window: [before next 1:1 / 3 days / 7 days]. Evidence: one line after, what did the prep change about how I listened?
