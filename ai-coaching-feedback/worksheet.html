My 1:1 prep card

The paper version of the capstone: one conversation, one prep move, one date. Roles, never names. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The conversation

Who is it with (role, not name), and what is it about?

2 · The prep move

Circle one: the rehearsal (AI plays the other side) · the SBI draft (my notes, de-identified) · the notes mine (my own 1:1 notes, roles only) · the themes move (survey themes into questions). Why this one?

3 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: names in unapproved tools · deliver an unverified draft · manage the conversation from a screen.

4 · The three SBI lines

Draft them now, behavior camera-true: the Situation, the Behavior, the Impact.

5 · The prep window

Circle one: before my next 1:1 · within 3 days · within 7 days. Which day, specifically?

6 · The evidence line

Fill in after the conversation: what did the prep change about how I listened?
