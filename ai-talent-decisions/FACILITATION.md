# Facilitation Guide · Talent Calls, Sharper

Full runbook in [`facilitator/notes.json`](facilitator/notes.json) on the
ATD framework. Regenerate after any change:

```bash
python3 tools/build-facilitator.py
```

**Pre-work:** bring one upcoming talent decision, held privately. **The
norm:** roles and rubrics, never names. **Partner rule:** anything
candidate-facing runs with HR; this session repeats it on purpose.

## Run of show, two paths

| Slide | Full (40 min) | Core (30 min) |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome / QR | 2 | 1 |
| Objectives | 1 | 1 |
| Agenda | 1 | skip |
| 01 Instruments, not verdicts | 8 | 6 |
| Manifesto | 1 | skip |
| 02 The Rubric Lab | 8 | 6 |
| 03 Skills, gaps, and growth | 8 | 6 |
| Recap quiz | 3 | 3 |
| My Talent Toolkit Card | 5 | 5 |
| Glossary | 1 | skip |
| Closing / commitment | 2 | 2 |
| **Total** | **40** | **30** |

**Core-path rules:** both trainers, the lab, the recap, and the capstone
stay; pair drills become solo passes.

**Safety rails:** live cases and vendor-tool specifics route to HR, never
adjudicated from the front of the room. The 'AI is less biased than
humans' argument gets the honest three-part answer in the contingencies.

## Validation model

Trainer scores and the graded lab in the moment; 4-question recap (L2) and
fist-to-five (L1) at close; the toolkit card plus the 7-day pulse on
instruments actually built (L3).
