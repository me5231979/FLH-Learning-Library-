Talent Calls, Sharper: the cheat sheet

The line

  • AI drafts instruments: rubrics, interview questions, skills taxonomies, succession profiles.
  • Humans make verdicts: hire, promote, rate, succeed. Every time.
  • The tell: the moment a draft starts ordering or scoring people, it became a verdict. Stop.

The data rules (strictest here)

  • Red: resumes, reviews, ratings, anything with a name. Never in unapproved tools.
  • Yellow: job descriptions, role criteria. Approved VU tools only.
  • Candidate-facing work runs with HR, in HR's systems, before AI touches it.
  • De-identify maps: roles and levels, never names beside scores.

The interview kit prompt (steal this)

"Here is a job description. Give me the five competencies this role actually needs, two behavioral questions per competency (tell me about a time...), and a three-level anchored scoring guide per question with a concrete example of a 1, a 2, and a 3. Then flag any criterion that is hard to observe in an interview."

Then the human passes: cut the unobservable, verify anchors against real answers, flag bias magnets, score independently.

The 9-box, made defensible

  1. Criteria from the roles' real outcomes, observable only.
  2. Anchors a stranger could apply (concrete examples per level).
  3. Independent scoring; differences discussed, never averaged away.
  4. AI drafts and stress-tests. It never places anyone.

Bias magnets (rewrite or cut)

  • "Culture fit" · "polish" · "executive presence": vague criteria that let similarity in dressed as standards.
  • The camera test: if a camera couldn't check it, it can't be a criterion.
  • Ask AI to flag the magnets in your final rubric.

Skills map recipe

  1. Taxonomy from the team's JDs (approved tool).
  2. Self-ratings: people are the source of truth on their own skills.
  3. De-identified map vs next year's goals; AI drafts the gap list.
  4. Per gap: grow · borrow · hire. Most closes are the first two.

Succession, done right

  • AI drafts the profile: capabilities, experiences, readiness criteria for the role.
  • It never names the successor, scores candidates, or reads reviews.
  • The succession call: conversations, evidence, and a signature with a name.

My toolkit card, fill in the blanks

The talent work: [the decision coming up]. First instrument: [interview kit / 9-box rubric / skills map / succession profile]. I will NOT: [let AI rank people / paste names or reviews into unapproved tools / keep unobservable criteria]. Build session: 45 min on [day], approved tools, human passes done. Standard: a stranger could score with it.
