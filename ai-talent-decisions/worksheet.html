My talent toolkit card

The paper version of the capstone: one instrument, one build session, one date. Roles and rubrics, never names.

1 · The talent work

What decision or cycle is coming up (role or process, never a person)?

2 · The first instrument

Circle one: interview kit from the JD · 9-box rubric with anchors · de-identified skills map · succession profile. Why this one?

3 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: let AI score or rank people · names or reviews into unapproved tools · keep criteria I can't observe.

4 · One anchor, drafted now

Pick one criterion and write its level-3 anchor: concrete, observable, appliable by a stranger.

5 · The build session

Circle one: tomorrow · within 3 days · within 7 days. Which day, and who else should be in it (role)?

6 · The evidence line

After the next talent decision: could you answer "why?" with anchors and examples?
