My Week Map

The paper version of the capstone: five blocks, each with its method and its check, one stack, and a named bank. Blocks and workflows, never people's names. This map is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The five blocks

Your week's recurring blocks, from last week's real calendar. One row each: the block, its honest hours, the method that fits its verb, its check.

BlockHoursMethod (by verb)The check

2 · The first stack

Circle one: minutes into drafts · numbers into slides · answers into a decision brief. Which block runs it, and what is the check between the links?

3 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: let saved time evaporate unnamed · run a chain without checks · put people data in unapproved tools while moving fast.

4 · The bank

Where do the reclaimed hours go, by name? The backlog project, the deep-work block, leaving on time. When does the bank block appear on the calendar, and who can see it?

5 · The calendar pass

Circle one: tomorrow · within 3 days · within 7 days. Which day, exactly, do you sit down with next week's calendar for 30 minutes?

6 · The traffic light check

Which of your five blocks touch yellow data (approved VU tools only)? Do any touch red (people data, never in unapproved tools)?

7 · Two weeks out, the evidence line

Fill in after the first two weeks: how many assists ran, what they saved, and what the banked time bought.
