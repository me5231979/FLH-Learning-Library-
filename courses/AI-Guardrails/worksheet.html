Our Four Lines agreement

The paper version of the team agreement: fill it in together, in one 20-minute conversation. Write lines the team said out loud, argue the hard cases while they're cheap, and give the finished page a home everyone can name.

The team and the work this covers

Whose work, which outputs (the newsletter, the FAQ, email replies, reports)?

Line 1 · What goes in

Which data may enter which tools? Traffic light: green public or generic, go · yellow internal work, approved VU tools only (ChatGPT EDU, Amplify, Copilot) · red private information about people, never. Name your team's hard cases and their color.

Line 2 · When a human verifies

Which outputs get a named check before they travel? Who checks, against what source, before it goes where? Numbers, names, dates, and policy claims always get the full source check.

Line 3 · When we disclose

Your default (circle one): university's voice or judgment of a person's work · everything external · misled test case by case. Then write your one-line disclosure wording.

Line 4 · Who owns it

Pre-written for every team: the human who ships it answers for it, one name per output, every word read. Add any output-to-owner pairings worth naming.

The agreement's home

One findable place: the team drive's front page, the wiki, the onboarding doc. Where does this page live?

The quarterly revisit

15 minutes: new cases, new questions, wording fixes. Which date is on the calendar?

Unclear cases go to

Name the routes: FERPA questions to the registrar's guidance · tool questions to IT's approved list · research and funder rules to the research office · anything else to whom?
