My repeat audit card

The paper version of the capstone: one task, one move, one date. Your tasks and templates, never other people's names. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The candidates

Three repeats from your own week: the message you send most often, the report you rebuild on a schedule, the request you always answer the same way.

2 · The chosen one

The one task you will automate this week, and what it costs you now (minutes per repeat, repeats per month).

3 · The move

Circle one: template it from three past examples · set up the three triage piles · add two calendar rules · test-retire one recurring item. Why this move?

4 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: auto-send anything · leave a name baked in a template · let the saved hour evaporate unnamed.

5 · The build session

Circle one: tomorrow · within 3 days · within 7 days. Which day, and where do the 20 quiet minutes come from?

6 · The bank

What is the recovered time FOR? Name the real work, and name the calendar block where it will live.

7 · Two weeks out, the evidence line

Fill in after the build: what does the task cost now, and what did the banked hours actually buy?
