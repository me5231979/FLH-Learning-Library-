My change card

The paper version of the capstone: one change, one conversation, one date. Situations and roles, never people's names. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The change

The AI change your team is facing, one line, and where the team stands on it today (excited, watchful, worried, mixed).

2 · The naming line

The fear said plainly, in your own words. Start from: "Some of you are wondering..." Write the sentence you would actually say.

3 · The honest frame

Three lines: what is changing, what is not, what you don't know yet.

4 · The job answer, both parts

What you know, and what you promise (role news from you first; the saved hours' named destination).

5 · The invitation

What the team gets to design: what gets tried first, what gets protected, what goes on the ethics session's agenda.

6 · The first move and its date

Circle one: naming conversation · job answer in the team meeting · ethics session · fear question in the next 1:1. Which day?

7 · The revisit date

Thirty days out: what's working, what isn't, what the tool can do now. Put the actual date here.

8 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: promise nothing will change · let enthusiasm silence the anxious · outsource the ethics conversation to a policy document. What's your counter-move?
