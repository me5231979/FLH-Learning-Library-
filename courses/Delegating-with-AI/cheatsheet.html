Delegation routing, the cheat sheet

The Routing Test, in order

  1. Growth: would doing this grow a person? If yes, stretch beats speed; the test is over and the person gets the rep.
  2. Judgment: does it need context, relationships, or accountability? Then it stays human, and sometimes stays yours.
  3. Volume: is it mechanical repetition with catchable errors? AI's lane, always with a named verifier.

Order rule: growth first, always. Volume is the loudest question and the least important.

Stretch vs grunt, the tells

  • Stretch: hard because it is NEW. Feedback attached; leaves a capability behind.
  • Grunt: hard because it is LONG. What did rep 30 teach that rep 3 did not? Nothing? Grunt.
  • The trap: yesterday's stretch (first drafts, research passes) is today's AI lane. Build replacement reps: verification ownership, client seats, judgment calls with feedback.
  • Removing grunt is a favor. Removing stretch is a theft.

The handoff brief: to a person

  1. Outcome: what done looks like.
  2. Context: why it matters and where it fits.
  3. Checkpoints: when you will look, agreed up front, spaced by stakes.
  4. Struggle room: no rescue at the first wobble. Struggle produces attempts; drowning produces silence. Check for attempts.

The handoff brief: to AI

  1. The draft assignment: what it produces, from which inputs, to what standard.
  2. The named verifier: who checks, against what source, with the power to reject. No verifier, no handoff.

A delegation to AI is really a delegation to the verifier. And either way, you own what ships.

The traffic light (data rule)

  • Green: public or generic information. Go.
  • Yellow: internal work. Approved VU tools only.
  • Red: private information about people. Never.

The light outranks the test: red data closes the AI lane, whatever the volume question says.

Role math, when AI absorbs a chunk

  1. Name what drains: the routine chunk, with a named verifier from day one.
  2. Name what backfills BEFORE announcing: judgment reps, client contact, verification ownership.
  3. Say what the freed capacity is for, out loud.

A role redesigned with its owner grows; a role shrunk from a distance starts job hunting.

The numbers, for the skeptics

Gallup: US employees using AI at work at least a few times a year nearly doubled in two years to about 40%; daily use doubled to about 8%; only about 22% say their organization has communicated a clear AI plan. Stanford GSB: the AI-era leader's job shifts to redesigning roles and processes rather than simply overseeing execution. The routing is already happening; the deciding is the part that is missing.

The 15-minute handoff conversation, fill in the blanks

Task: [the one you have been holding]. Destination: [AI + verifier / stretch / split / keep, with the reason]. Brief: [outcome, context, checkpoint] or [draft assignment + named verifier]. I will NOT: [route a learning rep away for speed / hand AI work unverified / keep from habit]. Check, two weeks out: [what the routing freed, what the person or verifier caught or learned].
