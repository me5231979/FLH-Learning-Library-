My adoption plan

The paper version of the capstone: one team, one first move, one date. Roles and rituals, never people's names. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · My team, and its AI temperature

Who are they, and where does each cluster sit today: enthusiasts, waiting-to-see, openly skeptical? One honest line.

2 · The first move

Circle one: narrate one real use in the next team meeting · run the permission talk · add the 1:1 question · start the miss log (and log the first miss myself). Why this one first?

3 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: use AI secretly while my team guesses the rules · leaderboard individuals · let month three go unmeasured.

4 · My permission talk, in three lines

Encouraged (with its check) · Never (starting from red: private information about people, never) · The gray zone and who to ask.

5 · The date

Circle one: tomorrow · within 3 days · within 7 days. Which day exactly, and in which meeting does it happen?

6 · The rituals and their cadence

Which ritual installs first, and on what weekly slot? (One at a time; four weeks before the next.)

7 · Day 30, the evidence line

Fill in after a month: who tried something new, what did the team catch before it shipped, and where did the recovered hours go?

8 · Week ten, the dip note

Write the calendar reminder now: which sustaining structure will wobble first on your team, and what's your seed move that week?
