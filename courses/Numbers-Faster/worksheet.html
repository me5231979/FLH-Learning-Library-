My Data Loop card

The paper version of the capstone: one dataset, one loop, one date. Datasets and columns, never names or personal records. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The dataset

Which spreadsheet or report gets the Data Loop this week, and what does answering its questions cost you by hand today (minutes or hours)?

2 · The light

Circle it: green (public or generic) · yellow (internal, no people; approved VU tools only) · red (people data; de-identify a copy first or keep it out). What does de-identifying take, if it applies?

3 · The describe line

One paragraph: what the file is, one row per what, the period, the source, the columns, the quirks.

4 · Three rungs of the ladder

Rung one: summarize what? Rung two: compare what against what? Rung three: which outlier or change to explain?

5 · The check plan

Which number gets recomputed by hand, what totals check applies, and which claim would earn a row trace?

6 · The first run

Circle one: tomorrow · within 3 days · within 7 days. Which day, and where do the minutes-to-answer numbers get written down?

7 · The two-sentence finding

Fill in after the run: sentence one, the change with its number; sentence two, why the reader should care. The recommendation is yours to add.
