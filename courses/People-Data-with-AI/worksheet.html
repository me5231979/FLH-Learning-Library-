My people-data card

The paper version of the capstone: one source, one move, one date. Patterns and roles, never people's names. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The source

Which people data will you work this month? (Survey results, a retro board, a pulse, your own 1:1 notes.) What has it been waiting for?

2 · The aggregation step

How does it get safe? Names out · identifying roles and events out · head count checked (under 5 in any slice stays out entirely). Which tool, from the approved VU list, sees the result?

3 · The three questions

Themes (with supporting quotes) · contrast with last cycle · the question behind the question ("what would explain both X and Y?"). Write your version of the third one; it does the most work.

4 · The humanize test

For each theme that comes back: one concrete example you have actually seen. No example? Write it here as a question for the team instead.

5 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: put a named person's data in any AI tool · let AI write the verdict on a person · treat small-team data as safe because it is technically aggregated.

6 · The conversation, scheduled

The data was collected for a conversation. Which one is it (share-back, 1:1 question, the postponed feedback), who is in it, and what day is it on the calendar?

7 · Two weeks out, the evidence line

Fill in after: which conversations happened, and what did they change?
