My verification card

The paper version of the capstone, and a working card for any single AI output: name it, underline it, trace it, question it, then give a verdict. Outputs and tasks, never private information about people. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The output

What is it, where does it go, and who will act on it? (That answer sets the budget: no check · claim read · all three reads · human expert.)

2 · The fault lines (claim read)

List every checkable claim: names, numbers, dates, quotes, citations. Circle the one that travels farthest and would cost most if wrong.

3 · The sources (source read)

For each circled claim: the source you opened, and whether it says what the output says. An unfindable source stops the card.

4 · The slant (slant read)

What is this output assuming? Who is missing from it? What would the strongest opposite case say?

5 · The verdict

Circle one: ship as is · fix and ship · stop using AI for this task. If stop: which stop rule fired (three misses · invented source · uncheckable judgment)?

6 · The miss tally

Misses on this task so far, with the failure mode of each (hallucination · staleness · slant · omission · faked math). Three in a row and the task comes back to you.

7 · Time spent checking

Minutes this card took, next to what it caught. That pair of numbers is what makes the habit feel worth keeping.
