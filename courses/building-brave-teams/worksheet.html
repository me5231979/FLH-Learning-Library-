Brave commitment card

The paper version of the capstone: one start, one stop, one question, one note, one rung, 14 days, one witness. Photograph it before you leave; the exit ticket is on the back.

1 · Start

One behavior you'll START in the next 14 days, something your team would see on camera.

2 · Stop

One behavior you'll STOP, steal it from your table's contract column.

3 · The question

Circle one to ask at your next team meeting: "What am I missing?" · "What would make it easier to speak up here?" · "What are we not saying?" · "Where did I shut a conversation down this week?" Then count to ten.

4 · Tomorrow's note

Who gets a note before end of day tomorrow? Circle the kind: thank-you for candor · blind-spot acknowledgment · invitation to disagree.

5 · The rung

Circle the stage you're building: Inclusion · Learner · Contributor · Challenger. What's your tell that it's working?

6 · The witness

Who sees this card today, and who checks on you at day 14?
