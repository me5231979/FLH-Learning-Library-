Difficult conversations, the cheat sheet

SBI: feedback that lands

  1. Situation: time and place, not "always." "In Monday's client review…"
  2. Behavior: what a camera would have seen. Not "you were careless."
  3. Impact: the concrete cost, no verdict. "The client saw two different totals."
  4. Then Intent (SBI-I): ask, don't guess. "What was going on for you?"

STATE: the safe opening

  1. Share the facts first, they're the least controversial start.
  2. Tell your story, owned as yours: "I'm starting to think…"
  3. Ask for their path: "How do you see it?"
  4. Talk tentatively: confidence in facts, humility in interpretation.
  5. Encourage testing: "If you see it differently, I want to hear it."

The three conversations

What Happened: the facts battle. Escape via the third story: "I think we see this differently."
Feelings: name yours once, plainly. Unnamed feelings leak into tone anyway.
Identity: the voice asking "what does this say about me?" It powers the avoidance. Answer it before you walk in.

Radical Candor: the grid

  • Radical Candor: care personally + challenge directly. The target.
  • Ruinous Empathy: care, no challenge. The most common manager miss.
  • Obnoxious Aggression: challenge, no care. Heard as an attack.
  • Manipulative Insincerity: neither. Praise up front, complaints behind backs.
  • Remember: candor is measured at the listener's ear, not your mouth.

Your opener, fill in the brackets

"I want to talk about [the topic], because I want [the outcome you both want]. Here's what I've noticed: in [situation], [the behavior a camera would see], and the impact was [the concrete cost]. I might not have the full picture. How do you see it?"

When it goes sideways

  • Tears: pause and acknowledge. Don't retract the message to stop them.
  • Anger: contrast statement: "I don't mean [wrong conclusion]. I do mean [your message]."
  • Silence: ask a real question, then count to six. Don't fill it first.
  • Safety gone: pause the meeting, keep the standard. "Let's pick this up tomorrow at 10."

Trust: the BRAVING check

  • Boundaries · Reliability · Accountability · Vault · Integrity · Nonjudgment · Generosity
  • Before a hard talk, ask: which of these have I banked with this person, and which have I broken?
  • People hear hard feedback only inside a circle of safety. Build it before you need it.

The commitment

Schedule it, private and unhurried, within 7 days. Tell one colleague you're having it, and tell them how it went after. Two minutes on the past, the rest on the future: "what would make the next one work?" A paused conversation resumed is a success. An avoided one just compounds.
