My conversation plan

The paper version of the capstone. Roles, not names. This page is yours; nobody collects it.

1 · The conversation

Who is it with (role, not name), and what is it about?

2 · SHRM's three questions, one line each

Where are we now? Where are we going? How do we get there?

3 · My opener

"I want to talk about [the topic], because I want [the outcome we both want]. Here's what I've noticed: in [situation], [the behavior a camera would see], and the impact was [the concrete cost]. I might not have the full picture. How do you see it?"
