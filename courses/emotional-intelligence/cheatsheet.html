Emotional intelligence, the cheat sheet

The four domains, in sequence

  1. Self-awareness: notice what you're feeling as it happens.
  2. Self-management: steer the reaction; the pause between stimulus and response.
  3. Social awareness: read the room; empathy as a skill, not a trait.
  4. Relationship management: where the first three pay off; trust built conversation by conversation.

Why the order matters: you can't manage what you haven't noticed.

Name it to tame it (90 seconds)

  1. Feel the spike.
  2. Name it, precisely and silently: "I'm feeling defensive because that landed as criticism."
  3. One breath.
  4. Choose the response.

The science: labeling reduces amygdala reactivity (UCLA, Lieberman 2007). Precision beats "stressed."

The listening rubric

  • Attention: eyes and body on the speaker; no devices.
  • Paraphrase: "so what I'm hearing is…" until they say "yes, that."
  • Judgment withheld: no advice, no "me too," no fixing, until asked.
  • The three-second rule: count three after they finish, before you speak.

The Johari move

  • 95% think they're self-aware; 10–15% are (Eurich).
  • Only feedback shrinks the Blind quadrant; introspection can't see what introspection can't see.
  • The one question to ask this week: "What's one thing I do that gets in my own way?"

The NVC reframe · OFNR

Observation a camera would agree with ("the last two meetings started without you") → Feeling you own ("I feel anxious," not "I feel ignored") → Need underneath ("I need to be able to plan reliably") → Request, specific, that survives hearing "no" ("could you message me if you'll be more than 5 minutes late?").

The two failure points: observations that are secretly judgments, and requests that are secretly demands.

Candor with care, three moves

  1. Name the shared goal: "we both want this launch to land."
  2. State the concern as an observation about the work, not the person.
  3. Invite their view as a genuine question.

Edmondson: psychological safety is not niceness. Safety + standards is the target.

The numbers, for the skeptics

  • EI ≈ 2× IQ + technical skill as an ingredient of star performance (Goleman, HBR).
  • Empathetic leaders: 40%+ higher at coaching and engaging (DDI).
  • Psychological safety: the strongest predictor in Google's Project Aristotle.
  • Respectful treatment outranks pay as a satisfaction driver (SHRM).
