My EI commitment card

The paper version of the capstone: one relationship, one practice, one date. Roles, never names — this card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The relationship or friction

Who is it with (role, not name), and what keeps happening?

2 · My practice

Circle one: the listening habit (paraphrase first) · the NVC reframe (OFNR) · candor with care · name it to tame it. Why this one, for this relationship?

3 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: interrupt · unsolicited advice · avoid the conversation entirely.

4 · My blind-spot question

Who will you ask this week: "What's one thing I do that gets in my own way?" (Role, not name.)

5 · First rep

Circle one: tomorrow · within 3 days · within 7 days. Which conversation, specifically?

6 · The evidence line

Fill in after the first rep: what went differently, in one line?

7 · Thirty days out

A month from now: has the relationship changed? What does the other person do differently around you?
