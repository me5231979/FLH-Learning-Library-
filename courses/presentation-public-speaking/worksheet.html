My presentation rebuild

The paper version of the capstone: one real upcoming presentation, rebuilt. Storyboard the beats before touching a single slide.

1 · The presentation

What is it, when is it, and who exactly is the audience?

2 · My Big Idea (one sentence)

[Point of view someone could oppose] + because + [what's at stake, with a number]. If it needs an "and also," split it.

3 · Structure, chosen for THIS room

Circle one: Sparkline (What Is ↔ What Could Be → New Bliss — hearts and minds) · SCQA (lead with the answer — decision, senior room). Why this one?

4 · The beats (storyboard before slides)

Sparkline: What Is → What Could Be → What Is → What Could Be → New Bliss. SCQA: Situation → Complication → Question → Answer (first!).

5 · My literal opening line

The exact first sentence out of your mouth. No apology, no warm-up. Say it out loud once before you leave the room.

6 · What I will CUT

Circle one: the bullet-wall slide · the apologetic opener · the ten-slide warm-up. What replaces it?

7 · The three feared questions

The pointed questions you're most afraid of, each with a one-sentence answer (bridge: acknowledge → answer → return).

8 · Rehearsal, on the calendar

Out loud, timed, once with one honest human. Which day, and who's the human?

9 · The evidence line

Fill in after the talk: did the opening land? Did the structure hold under questions?
