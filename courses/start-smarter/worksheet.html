My first try

The paper version of the capstone: one real chore, one prompt, one day. Fill it in, then go do it: it takes under five minutes.

1 · The chore

One real task from your actual week (the one you always put off works best).

2 · The superpower

Circle one: Draft it · Summarize it · Brainstorm it · Explain it · Rewrite it.

3 · The color

Circle one: 🟢 green (public/generic) · 🟡 yellow (internal: approved VU tools only) · 🔴 red (private info about people: keep it out, or remove the names).

4 · My prompt

The job + your details + the shape. Write the actual sentence you'll paste.

5 · When

Circle one: today · tomorrow morning · this week (write the day).

6 · After: what happened?

One line. Did the follow-up make it better? What did you check before using it?

The two moves to remember

1. If the answer is close: add one detail and ask again. 2. If it's important: check it at the source before you use it.
