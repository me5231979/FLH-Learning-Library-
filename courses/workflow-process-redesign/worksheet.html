My redesign card

The paper version of the capstone: one workflow, one draft step, one date. Workflows and roles, never people's names. This card is for your eyes, so be honest on it.

1 · The workflow

What repeating work is it, and what does it cost today (hours per week, and whose)?

2 · The first draft step

Circle one: the gather step (pulling and formatting inputs) · the transform step (first written pass) · a summary step (volume into themes) · map first. Why this step?

3 · What I will NOT do

Circle your honest failure mode: let AI drift into the decide step · run drafts without a named verifier · announce it as an efficiency play.

4 · The verify design

Four lines: who owns the check, what they check against, how often, what happens on a miss.

5 · The mapping session

Circle one: tomorrow · within 3 days · within 7 days. Which day, and who from the team must be in the room?

6 · The metric

Hours saved AND errors caught. Where will it be posted so the team can see it?

7 · Two weeks out, the evidence line

Fill in after the pilot: what does the workflow cost now, and what did the checks catch?
