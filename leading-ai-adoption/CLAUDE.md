Short-format course (Classroom 40 / Core 30 / self-paced ~15). Live at
https://me5231979.github.io/Course_Library/leading-ai-adoption/ (GitHub Pages, `gh-pages`
branch). Part of the CHART Program and Manager Voyage; catalog at

Learning Series courses.

## Course-specific principles (do not regress)

1. **Modeling over mandating.** Every interaction reinforces: visible,
   honest, specific use (the narrated use: use, check, miss). No content
   may endorse usage quotas or prompt-counting.
2. **The three guardrails** (data, sign-off, ownership) are the course's
   structure; the data rail IS the traffic light from Start Smarter, verbatim,
   and outranks everything.
3. **The work audit is the signature interactive** (user-requested):
   five dimensions, scored /12, moves ranked by weakest dimension,
   quarterly cadence. Its scoring values and tier thresholds
   (10+/6-9/<=5) are load-bearing; keep the audit private (nothing saved
   or sent) and say so in learner-visible copy.
4. **AI in coaching conversations is development, never surveillance**;
   the 1:1 question is the approved form.
5. Citations: Gallup 2026 (modeling + integration as top drivers),
   Accenture (~18 percent; curiosity, courage, connection). Directional
   claims only; no invented statistics.
