# Facilitation Guide · Leading the Shift

Full runbook in [`facilitator/notes.json`](facilitator/notes.json) on the
ATD framework. Regenerate after any change:

```bash
python3 tools/build-facilitator.py
```

**Facilitator rule:** you open section 01 with YOUR narrated AI use (use,
check, miss); this session's credibility runs on modeling the modeling.
**The norm:** teams and roles, never names; audits stay on learners'
screens.

**Core-path rules:** both trainers, the audit, the recap, and the capstone
stay; pair drills become solo passes. Never cut the audit.

**Safety rails:** normalize low audit scores instantly (most teams are in
shadow adoption); tool debates route to CHART resources; AI in reviews is
development, never surveillance.

## Validation model

Trainer scores and the completed audit in the moment; 4-question recap
(L2) and fist-to-five (L1) at close; the adoption plan plus the 7-day
pulse on first visible moves made and the 30-day audit rerun (L3).
