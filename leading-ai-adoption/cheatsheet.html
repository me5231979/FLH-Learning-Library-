Leading the Shift: the cheat sheet

What actually predicts adoption

  • Manager modeling: the team watches you use it, checks included (Gallup's top human predictor).
  • Workflow integration: AI inside real repeated work, with checks.
  • Tools, budgets, and mandates barely move the needle on their own.
  • Only about 18% of leaders do this well (Accenture): curiosity, courage, connection.

The narrated use (3 sentences)

  1. What I used AI for this week.
  2. What the check caught.
  3. What I'd do differently.

Rules: mundane beats impressive; misses welcome; ninety seconds at the team meeting.

The three guardrails (one page, written with the team)

Data: green (public or generic) go · yellow (internal) approved VU tools only · red (private information about people) never. Sign-off: outbound, high-stakes, or about people gets human review before it moves. Ownership: you own what you ship, whoever drafted it. Plus: amnesty for existing shortcuts, and a quarterly revision date.

Show your prompts (monthly, 15 min)

  1. Real uses, real prompts, real misses.
  2. Manager goes first, every time.
  3. Steal shamelessly; log nothing.

The 1:1 question

"What have you tried with AI lately, and what did you learn?"

Asked every 1:1. Development, never surveillance: no quotas, no counting, honest misses celebrated.

The AI-enabled work audit (quarterly, score /12)

1. Our three most repeated outputs: [list them]. 2. Where AI shows up: nowhere (0) · shortcuts (1) · one designed workflow (2) · several (3). 3. Team last SAW me use it: this week with checks (3) · this month (2) · never seen (1) · never (0). 4. Guardrails: written and posted (3) · verbal once (1) · guesses (0). 5. AI in coaching conversations: yes (3) · ad hoc (1) · never (0). Lowest dimension = next move. Rising scores = the adoption metric.

My adoption plan, fill in the blanks

Work I model on: [task + what AI does + my check]. First visible move: [narrated use / show-your-prompts / guardrails session / 1:1 question]. I will NOT: [mandate without modeling / leave rails unwritten / hide the misses]. Date: [next team meeting / 7 days / 14 days]. Metric: rerun the audit in a month; which dimension moved?
