The privacy note.

What you type

Course exercises — reflections, drafts, planners, commitment cards — run entirely in your browser. The text you type is never sent to a server, never stored by the site, and never visible to a facilitator or anyone else. It exists only on your screen; when you close the tab, it is gone. If a course offers a printable takeaway, printing happens from your own device through your browser's print dialog.

What the site stores on your device

Some courses remember which pages you have marked done, and the catalog remembers a display preference. These are small flags in your browser's local storage on your own device — a checkmark per page, no content. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Page analytics

When this site is served from its Vercel address, Vercel Web Analytics records anonymous page-view counts so the program can see which courses are used. It does not receive anything you type. When the site is served from GitHub Pages, no analytics load at all.

Classroom sessions

Classroom exercises are designed to be private by default: prompts ask for roles and workflows, never names, and share-outs are voluntary. What you type on your device during a session follows the same rule as above — it stays on your device.

Questions
